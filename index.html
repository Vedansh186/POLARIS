Theory, made visible

What the laboratory is showing

01

Unpolarized light

An unpolarized beam is an ensemble of waves with randomly distributed electric-field directions in the transverse plane—not one wave pointing in all directions at once.

02

Linear polarizer

An ideal first polarizer selects one field direction. Averaged across the random ensemble, it transmits half of the incident intensity: I₁ = I₀/2.

03

Malus’ Law

For each following polarizer, only the field projection on its axis passes. Because intensity is proportional to amplitude squared, I = I₀ cos²θ.
